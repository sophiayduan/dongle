# Submission Requirements
In short, your repository needs to clearly **show off what your project is** whilst including your source files.

`README.md` should include:
- A description of what your project is, and why you made it
- Screenshots of your Final assembly (PCB + case if applicable), PCB, schematic, and anything else you'd like (e.g. a poster, banner, pinout diagram)
- A Bill of Materials (BOM) with links, quantity, cost, and shipping. Even if you already own a part, include it!
  
### **Your repository should include:**
#### **PCB source files:**
- For KiCAD that includes at least `.kicad_pro`, `.kicad_sch`, `.kicad_pcb`, and `.kicad_prl`
#### **Case source files:**
Your project must have a complete CAD assembly, will all components (electronics included!)
- Upload `STEP Files (*.step)` for each item you've modelled
- Upload the source design file, the complete assembly (.f3d, FCStd, or a link to onshape)
#### **Minimal firmware (if applicable):**
- If you are using a MCU, you likely need firmware. Learn to program our tutorial dongle [here](https://dongle.hackclub.com/guide) 
- The firmware doesn't need to be tested nor complete, but should be a starting point for future development
	
  
Finally, *your project should be original, designed by you.* Not an AI agent, nor someone else, just you. Your project should be unique! It shouldn't be a 1-to-1 copy of any guide, add your own twist on it!
